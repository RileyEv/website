---
title: Lion Bar Rocky Road
date: 2025-08-03
image: /images/recipes/lion-bar-rocky-road.png

servings: 20 squares

description: A chewy, crunchy no-bake traybake packed with biscuits, marshmallows, and gooey Lion Bars — topped with sliced Lion Bar cross-sections for an irresistible finish. Perfect for a Lion Bar themed party.

ingredients:
  - 200g milk chocolate
  - 100g dark chocolate
  - 100g unsalted butter
  - 3 tbsp golden syrup
  - 4 Lion Bars, chopped
  - 100g digestive biscuits, broken into chunks
  - 75g mini marshmallows
  - 50g Cornflakes
  - "Optional: Extra Lion Bar halves, sliced lengthways to expose cross-section"
  - "Optional: White chocolate or caramel drizzle for topping"

instructions:
  - Line a 20cm square tin with baking paper.
  - In a large bowl, mix chopped Lion Bars, broken digestives, marshmallows, and Cornflakes.
  - In a saucepan over low heat, melt the chocolate, butter, and golden syrup until smooth. Let cool for 5 minutes.
  - Pour the melted mixture over the dry ingredients and stir quickly to combine.
  - Press the mixture firmly into the lined tin.
  - Top with halved Lion Bars, cut lengthways to show the caramel and wafer inside.
  - (Optional) Drizzle with melted white chocolate or caramel.
  - Chill for at least 2 hours, preferably overnight, until firm.
  - Slice into squares and serve.

notes: These bars improve with time — store in an airtight container in the fridge and they’ll stay chewy and delicious for up to 5 days. Perfect for transporting to work or slicing ahead of a party!
---

This Lion Bar Rocky Road is a nostalgic crowd-pleaser, no fuss, just pure indulgent joy. The halved Lion Bars on top give it a signature look that stands out at bake sales, potlucks, or your office treat table.
